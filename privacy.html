Privacy Policy

Privacy information for ATS Resume Builder, developed by JK Tech Studio.

Effective date: May 28, 2026

JK Tech Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use ATS Resume Builder.

01 Information You Create

ATS Resume Builder lets you create resumes, preview PDFs, use PDF tools, and check resume strength. Resume details you enter are stored locally on your device or browser storage so you can edit them later.

02 How Data Is Used

Resume information is used inside the app to generate resumes, previews, downloads, sharing files, and ATS scoring. We do not require an account, and we do not sell your resume information.

03 Local Storage

Saved resumes may be removed if you clear app data, clear browser storage, reset your device, or uninstall the app. Please keep your own backup copies of important resumes.

04 Ads and Third Parties

The app may show ads through Google Mobile Ads. Ad providers may collect device, advertising, and usage information according to their own policies. The app may also use platform services for sharing, file picking, and opening email.

05 Files and Permissions

When you choose files or images, the app uses them only for the action you request, such as converting images to PDF, merging PDFs, or saving a generated resume.

06 Children

This app is intended for general productivity use and is not directed to children. Do not enter private information for another person without permission.

07 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
